# Slimestore Orders

## Placing an Order

### How to Order
- Browse products on our website
- Add items to your cart
- Click "Checkout" when ready
- Enter shipping and payment information
- Review and confirm your order
- You'll receive a confirmation email immediately

### Guest Checkout
- No account required to place an order
- Just enter your email for order updates
- Create an account anytime to track orders easily

### Account Checkout
- Save your shipping addresses
- View order history
- Faster checkout for future orders
- Access to exclusive member deals

## Order Confirmation

### What to Expect
- Confirmation email sent within 5 minutes
- Contains order number, items, and total
- Check spam folder if not received
- Contact support if no email after 1 hour

### Order Number
- Format: SL-XXXXXX (e.g., SL-482951)
- Needed for tracking and support inquiries
- Found in confirmation email and account dashboard

## Modifying Orders

### Can I Change My Order?
- Contact us within 1 hour of placing order
- We can change shipping address
- We can add or remove items
- We can upgrade shipping method
- Changes not possible once order is shipped

### How to Request Changes
- Email: orders@slimestore.com
- Include order number and requested changes
- We'll confirm changes via email

## Cancelling Orders

### Cancellation Policy
- Free cancellation within 2 hours of ordering
- Contact us immediately to cancel
- Once shipped, cancellation not possible
- Return the order instead if already shipped

### How to Cancel
- Email orders@slimestore.com with order number
- Subject line: "Cancel Order SL-XXXXXX"
- Refund processed within 3-5 business days

## Order Status

### Status Types
- **Confirmed** - Order received, processing begins
- **Processing** - Order being prepared
- **Shipped** - Order dispatched, tracking available
- **Out for Delivery** - Arriving today
- **Delivered** - Successfully delivered
- **On Hold** - Issue with order (we'll contact you)

### Checking Order Status
- Log into your account
- Click "My Orders"
- Or use tracking link in shipping email

## Order Issues

### Wrong Item Received
- Contact us within 7 days
- Provide photos of wrong item
- We'll ship correct item immediately
- No need to return wrong item

### Missing Items
- Check if order was split into multiple packages
- Wait for all packages to arrive
- Contact us if item still missing after all deliveries
- We'll reship or refund missing items

### Damaged in Transit
- Take photos of damaged package and product
- Contact us within 7 days of delivery
- Full refund or replacement at no cost
- No need to return damaged items

## Bulk & Wholesale Orders

### Bulk Discounts
- Orders of 20+ items: 10% off

- Contact wholesale@slimestore.com for custom quotes
