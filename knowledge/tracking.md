# Slimestore Order Tracking

## How to Track Your Order

### Method 1: Tracking Link
- Check your shipping confirmation email
- Click the tracking link provided
- View real-time delivery status

### Method 2: Account Dashboard
- Log into your Slimestore account
- Go to "My Orders"
- Click on your order
- Click "Track Package"

### Method 3: Carrier Website
- Copy tracking number from email
- Visit carrier website (USPS, UPS, FedEx)
- Enter tracking number
- View detailed tracking

## Understanding Tracking Status

### Status Meanings
- **Label Created** - Shipping label printed, package not yet picked up
- **Picked Up** - Package collected by carrier
- **In Transit** - Package moving through carrier network
- **Out for Delivery** - Package on delivery truck, arriving today
- **Delivered** - Package successfully delivered
- **Delivery Attempted** - Carrier tried but couldn't deliver
- **Exception** - Delay or issue (see details)

### Common Delays
- Weather delays
- High carrier volume (holidays)
- Address verification needed
- Customs processing (international)

## Tracking FAQs

### Why isn't my tracking updating?
- Allow 24-48 hours after shipment for first scan
- Rural areas may have less frequent updates
- Weekends may show no movement
- Contact us if no updates after 5 days

### Package shows delivered but I didn't receive it
- Check around your property (porch, garage, bushes)
- Ask neighbors or building manager
- Check with family members
- Wait 24 hours (sometimes marked early)
- Contact us if still not found

### Can I change delivery address after shipping?
- Contact the carrier directly with tracking number
- Some carriers allow delivery holds/redirects
- Additional fees may apply
- We cannot change address once shipped

### Can I request a specific delivery date?
- Select shipping speed at checkout
- Overnight for next-day delivery
- We cannot guarantee specific dates for standard shipping
- Carrier may allow scheduling after shipment

## Delivery Options

### Leave Package Instructions
- Add delivery instructions at checkout
- "Leave at back door"
- "Ring doorbell"
- "Leave with neighbor"

### Signature Required
- Orders over $100 require signature
- Request signature for any order in checkout
- Carrier will attempt redelivery if missed

### Hold at Location
- Have package held at carrier facility
- Pick up at your convenience
- Contact carrier after shipment

## International Tracking

### International Shipments
- Tracking available worldwide
- May transfer between carriers at border
- Use both origin and destination carrier tracking
- Customs may cause tracking gaps

### Customs Delays
- Packages may be held for inspection
- Can take 1-5 additional days
- Duties/taxes may be required
- Contact local customs for holds

## Lost Packages

### If Your Package is Lost
- Wait until after expected delivery date
- Check tracking for any updates
- Contact us with order number
- We'll file a claim with carrier
- Reship or refund provided at no cost

### Stolen Packages
- File a police report
- Contact us with report number
- We'll work with you on resolution
- Consider requiring signature for future orders
