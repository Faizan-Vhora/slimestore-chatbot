# Slimestore Returns & Refunds

## Return Policy

### Eligibility
- Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery
- Product must be unused and in original packaging
- Opened slimes cannot be returned for hygiene reasons
- Defective products can be returned even if opened

### Non-Returnable Items
- Opened slime containers (unless defective)
- DIY kits with broken seals
- Sale items marked "Final Sale"
- Gift cards

## How to Return

### Step 1: Request Return
- Log into your account or contact customer support
- Provide order number and reason for return
- Wait for return authorization (within 24 hours)

### Step 2: Ship Item
- Pack item securely in original packaging
- Include return authorization number
- Ship to address provided in authorization email
- Customer pays return shipping unless item is defective

### Step 3: Receive Refund
- Refund processed within 5-7 business days of receiving return
- Refund issued to original payment method
- Shipping costs are non-refundable (unless our error)

## Refund Options

### Full Refund
- Available for unopened products returned within 30 days
- Defective products qualify for full refund including shipping

### Store Credit
- Available for any return
- Store credit never expires
- Bonus 10% added to store credit (vs. refund)

### Exchange
- Exchange for different product at no extra cost
- If new item costs more, pay the difference
- If new item costs less, receive store credit

## Defective Products

### What Qualifies as Defective
- Slime arrived dried out or contaminated
- Wrong product received
- Product damaged during shipping
- Product doesn't match description

### Defective Product Process
- Contact us within 7 days of delivery
- Provide photos of the issue
- We'll send replacement or full refund
- No need to return defective item

## Contact for Returns
- Email: returns@slimestore.com
- Response time: Within 24 hours
- Include order number in all communications
