# Slimestore Policies

## Privacy Policy

### Information We Collect
- Name and contact information
- Shipping and billing addresses
- Payment information (processed securely, not stored)
- Order history
- Website usage data (cookies)

### How We Use Information
- Process and ship orders
- Send order updates and tracking
- Improve our products and services
- Send marketing emails (with consent)

### Information Sharing
- We never sell your personal information
- Share with shipping carriers for delivery only
- Share with payment processors for transactions
- May share if required by law

### Your Rights
- Request copy of your data
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of marketing emails
- Update your information anytime

## Safety Information

### Product Safety
- All slimes are non-toxic
- Suitable for ages 5 and up
- Adult supervision recommended for young children
- Not intended for consumption
- Keep away from pets

### Allergen Information
- Some products contain gluten
- Products made in facility with common allergens
- Contact us for specific ingredient questions
- Discontinue use if skin irritation occurs
