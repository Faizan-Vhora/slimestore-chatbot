# Slimestore Account

## Creating an Account

### How to Sign Up
- Click "Sign Up" on our website
- Enter email and create password
- Verify email address
- Account ready to use

### Benefits of Having an Account
- Track all your orders in one place
- Save multiple shipping addresses
- Faster checkout experience
- Order history access
- Exclusive member discounts
- Early access to new products
- Wishlist feature

### Account is Free
- No cost to create an account
- No subscription or fees
- Cancel anytime

## Logging In

### How to Log In
- Click "Login" on website
- Enter email and password
- Click "Sign In"

### Login Issues
- Make sure caps lock is off
- Check for typos in email
- Use "Forgot Password" if needed
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try a different browser

### Forgot Password
- Click "Forgot Password" on login page
- Enter your email address
- Check email for reset link (within 5 minutes)
- Click link and create new password
- Link expires in 24 hours

## Managing Your Account

### Update Personal Information
- Log into your account
- Go to "Account Settings"
- Edit name, email, or phone
- Click "Save Changes"

### Change Password
- Go to "Account Settings"
- Click "Change Password"
- Enter current password
- Enter new password twice
- Click "Update Password"

### Shipping Addresses
- Save multiple addresses
- Set a default address
- Add, edit, or delete addresses
- Name each address (Home, Work, etc.)

### Payment Methods
- We don't store full card numbers
- Payment processed securely each time
- PayPal and Apple Pay remember your info

## Email Preferences

### Marketing Emails
- Opt in or out anytime
- Go to "Email Preferences" in account
- Choose what emails you want:
  - New product announcements
  - Sale notifications
  - Newsletter
  - Order updates (always sent)

### Unsubscribe
- Click "Unsubscribe" in any marketing email
- Or update preferences in account settings
- Order-related emails cannot be unsubscribed

## Order History

### Viewing Past Orders
- Log into account
- Click "My Orders"
- See all orders with status
- Click any order for details

### Reorder Previous Items
- Go to order history
- Click "Reorder" on any past order
- Items added to cart
- Update quantities if needed

## Wishlist

### How to Use Wishlist
- Click heart icon on any product
- View wishlist in your account
- Move items to cart when ready
- Share wishlist with friends/family

### Wishlist Notifications
- Get notified when wishlist items go on sale
- Get notified if item is low in stock
- Enable in account settings

## Deleting Your Account

### How to Delete Account
- Email support@slimestore.com
- Subject: "Delete My Account"
- Include email address used for account
- Account deleted within 48 hours

### What Happens When Deleted
- All personal information removed
- Order history no longer accessible
- Cannot be undone
- Active orders will still be delivered

## Account Security

### Keeping Your Account Safe
- Use a strong, unique password
- Don't share your login details
- Log out on shared computers
- Contact us if suspicious activity
