# Slimestore Payment Information

## Accepted Payment Methods

### Credit & Debit Cards
- Visa
- Mastercard
- American Express
- Discover
- All transactions are secure and encrypted

### Digital Wallets
- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- PayPal
- Shop Pay

### Other Payment Options
- Gift Cards (Slimestore gift cards)
- Store Credit
- Afterpay (Buy now, pay in 4 installments)
- Klarna (Pay in 4 or monthly financing)

## Payment Security

### Security Features
- 256-bit SSL encryption on all transactions
- PCI DSS compliant
- No card information stored on our servers
- Secure checkout powered by Stripe

### Fraud Protection
- All orders screened for fraud
- Billing address verification required
- CVV verification on all card transactions
- Suspicious orders may require additional verification

## Billing Information

### When You're Charged
- Card charged when order is placed
- PayPal charged immediately
- Afterpay/Klarna first payment at checkout

### Order Confirmation
- Email confirmation sent immediately after purchase
- Receipt includes itemized list and total
- Save confirmation for your records

## Payment Issues

### Declined Transactions
- Verify card details are correct
- Ensure sufficient funds available
- Contact your bank if issues persist
- Try alternative payment method

### Double Charges
- Check if order was placed twice
- Pending charges usually resolve in 3-5 days
- Contact support with transaction details for help

## Discounts & Promotions

### Applying Promo Codes
- Enter code at checkout
- Only one promo code per order
- Some exclusions may apply
- Codes cannot be applied after purchase

### Gift Cards
- Purchase in amounts from $10-$100
- Never expire
- Can be combined with other payment methods
- Check balance on our website

## Currency
- All prices in USD
- International orders converted at checkout
- Currency conversion fees may apply (check with your bank)
