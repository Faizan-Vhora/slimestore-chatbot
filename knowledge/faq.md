# Slimestore Frequently Asked Questions

## Product Questions

### How long does slime last?
With proper care, our slimes last 2-3 months. Store in airtight container in a cool, dry place. If slime gets sticky, add a small amount of contact lens solution.

### Is slime safe for kids?
Yes! All our slimes are non-toxic and safe for ages 5 and up. We recommend adult supervision for younger children. Slime is not edible and should be kept away from pets.

### What if my slime dries out?
Add a few drops of water or lotion and knead well. If slime is too sticky, add a tiny bit of contact lens solution. Store properly to prevent drying.

### Can I customize my slime order?
Yes! Add glitter, foam beads, or scent oils to customize. Select options during checkout or purchase add-ins separately. Custom orders available for 3+ slimes.

### What's the difference between slime types?
- Butter slime: Smooth, spreadable, soft texture
- Cloud slime: Light, fluffy, airy feel
- Clear slime: Transparent, great bubble pops
- Floam: Crunchy texture with foam beads
- Glossy slime: Shiny and stretchy
- Bingsu slime: Crunchy with small beads

### What size slime should I get?
- Mini (2 oz): Try new types, party favors
- Regular (4 oz): Best value, standard play
- Large (8 oz): Slime enthusiasts
- Jumbo (16 oz): Parties, sharing

### Are your slimes scented?
Most slimes have light, pleasant scents. Unscented options available. Scents include vanilla, strawberry, lavender, cotton candy, and more.

### Can slime be shared between kids?
Yes, but we recommend washing hands before and after play. Each child could have their own container for hygiene.
