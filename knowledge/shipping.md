# Slimestore Shipping Information

## Shipping Options

### Standard Shipping
- **Cost**: $4.99 for orders under $35, FREE for orders $35+
- **Delivery Time**: 5-7 business days
- **Available**: Continental United States only

### Express Shipping
- **Cost**: $9.99
- **Delivery Time**: 2-3 business days
- **Available**: Continental United States only

### Overnight Shipping
- **Cost**: $19.99
- **Delivery Time**: Next business day (order by 2 PM EST)
- **Available**: Continental United States only

### International Shipping
- **Cost**: Starting at $14.99 (varies by location)
- **Delivery Time**: 10-21 business days
- **Available**: Canada, UK, Australia, EU countries
- International orders may be subject to customs fees

## Shipping Policies

### Order Processing
- Orders placed before 2 PM EST ship same business day
- Orders placed after 2 PM EST ship next business day
- Orders are not processed on weekends or holidays

### Tracking
- All orders include free tracking
- Tracking number sent via email within 24 hours of shipment
- Track your order on our website or carrier's site

### Packaging
- All slimes are securely packaged to prevent leaks
- Orders ship in eco-friendly packaging
- Heat packs included during cold months (Nov-Feb) at no extra cost

## Shipping Restrictions
- We cannot ship to PO Boxes for express/overnight orders
- Some products may have shipping restrictions to certain states
- Hazardous weather may cause delays (we'll notify you)

## Lost or Damaged Packages
- Report issues within 7 days of expected delivery
- We will reship or refund at no cost
- Photo documentation required for damage claims

## Holiday Shipping
- Extended processing times during peak seasons
- Order by December 15th for Christmas delivery (standard shipping)
- Order by December 20th for Christmas delivery (express shipping)
